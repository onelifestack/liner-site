Press kit

Liner is a free, private music player for Android and the web. For anything else, write to hello@onelifestack.com.

In one line

Liner plays the music already on your phone, with liner notes, synced lyrics, karaoke and six skins, and keeps everything on the device.

Facts

  • Name: Liner
  • Maker: OneLifeStack
  • Platform: Android 8.0 and newer; a web version for laptops
  • Price: free, no ads, no purchases
  • Audience: ages 13 and over
  • Privacy: no account, no analytics, no internet permission
  • Beta: 0.9.0-beta.1 (Android APK) at liner.onelifestack.com/#beta, SHA-256 71132de1555b189dbcf63bad5b6af9e8b7efcfa5d55007fb28c367f34eda04e9
