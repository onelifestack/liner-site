Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Liner is a music player that works only on your phone. This page says what it does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

What Liner reads

  • Your audio files. With your permission, Liner reads the songs on your phone (title, artist, album, cover art, length) so it can build your shelves and play them. It reads files in folders you add, and lyric files (.lrc) beside them.
  • Nothing else. Liner does not read your photos, messages, contacts or documents.

What Liner stores, on your phone only

  • A list of your songs, your play counts and loved songs, your liner notes, your collections, lyrics you added, and your settings.
  • Karaoke takes you choose to save.
  • These stay in Liner's private storage. Android does not back them up to the cloud. You can make a backup file yourself (Settings, Back up to a file) and keep it where you like.

The microphone

Liner asks for the microphone only for Karaoke mode, and only records while you tap Record my take. Takes stay on your phone until you delete them. Liner does not listen at any other time.

Notifications

The now-playing notification shows what is playing and has play, pause and skip buttons. It is not used for anything else.

No internet, no accounts, no ads

Liner does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. (The playback library adds a permission that lets an app see whether a network exists. Liner never uses it to connect.) There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. We do not collect, sell or share data about you because we never receive any.

Your choices

  • Turn off any permission in Android settings at any time. Liner will show an empty shelf until you allow music access again.
  • Settings, Erase everything removes all of Liner's data on this phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • Hiding a song in Liner never deletes the file.

Children

Liner is suitable for ages 13 and over. It collects no information from anyone.
