Support

We are a small team and we read every message.

Write to us

Email hello@onelifestack.com. In the app, Settings > Help > Write to us opens your email app with the app and Android versions filled in, never your music, and you can read and edit it before sending.

Common questions

Is Liner free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

Where does Liner get my music?

From the audio files already on your phone: the Music and Downloads folders, plus any folder you add. It never downloads or streams anything.

Does it need the internet?

No. Liner does not even ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

How do lyrics work?

Put a .lrc file next to a song in a folder you added, or add one from the song's Lyrics screen, or paste the words. Liner shows them in time with the music. It does not look lyrics up online.

How does Karaoke lower the singer's voice?

A singer sits in the middle of a stereo mix, so Liner turns down what both speakers share. A little bass may go with it, and mono songs cannot be treated. It is a guide, not a studio tool.

What if I change phones?

Use Settings > Back up to a file to save your notes, loved songs, collections, lyrics and settings. Copy the file and your music to the new phone and choose Restore. Songs are matched by title, artist and album.

Is there a web version?

Yes, for laptops: open it in Chrome, Edge or Brave, pick a music folder, and it plays in the browser. Backup files work between the phone and the web.

How do I install the beta?

Open the Beta section on your phone, tap Download, and open the file when it finishes. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app once; say yes, then tap Install. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer.

How do I get help or share an idea?

Open Settings > Help in the app, or write to hello@onelifestack.com.

How do I delete everything?

Settings > Erase everything in the app, or just uninstall it. There is nothing to delete anywhere else, and your music files are never touched.

Something went wrong?

If Liner ever closes unexpectedly, Settings > Help > Tell us what happened appears with the error and the versions only, never your music.
